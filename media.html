01 / SHORT BIO

Hanna Laikin

Hanna Laikin is a Los Angeles-based entrepreneur, Co-Founder & Head of Product at NOCI, founder of BrightPoint Communications, and host of The Hustle by Hanna.

Her work spans fashion, consumer technology, communications and media. A graduate of USC Marshall, Hanna has built her career across entertainment, clean energy, brand strategy, product and entrepreneurship.
