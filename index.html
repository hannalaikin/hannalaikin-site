01 / ABOUT

A career built
across categories.

Hanna Laikin is a Los Angeles-based entrepreneur and operator working across fashion, consumer technology, communications and media.

She is Co-Founder & Head of Product at NOCI, founder of BrightPoint Communications, and creator and host of The Hustle by Hanna. Her work blends product thinking, brand strategy and storytelling with a bias toward actually building.

Before founding and operating her current businesses, Hanna worked across entertainment, technology and clean energy after moving from Indiana to Los Angeles to attend the University of Southern California.
